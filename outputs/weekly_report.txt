**Executive Summary**

Our e-commerce platform is currently serving a total of 96,478 customers, with 7,781 identified as our most valuable 'Champions'. However, a significant portion of our customer base, 39,822, is deemed 'At Risk', indicating a potential decline in their engagement and loyalty. Furthermore, a substantial 68,837 customers have already churned, highlighting the need for targeted retention strategies. Despite these challenges, our demand forecast suggests a steady stream of orders, with an expected 301 orders per day over the next 30 days. This indicates a relatively stable business health, but with areas that require attention and improvement.

**Key Risks and Alerts**
Our anomaly detection systems have flagged 149 sellers for suspicious activity, which may be contributing to subpar customer experiences. Notably, our worst delivery delay detected is 35.0 days, which is significantly higher than acceptable standards. These issues, combined with the high number of 'At Risk' and churned customers, signal potential risks to our reputation, customer satisfaction, and ultimately, our revenue. It is essential that we address these problems promptly to prevent further deterioration and maintain a competitive edge in the market. The operations team must prioritize these alerts and develop strategies to mitigate their impact on our business.

**Recommended Actions**
To address the identified risks and improve our overall business health, we recommend the following actions for the operations team: (1) conduct a thorough investigation into the anomalous seller activity and take corrective measures to prevent similar issues in the future; (2) collaborate with our logistics partners to reduce delivery delays and establish more reliable shipping timelines; (3) develop targeted marketing campaigns and retention strategies to re-engage 'At Risk' customers and prevent further churn; and (4) continuously monitor our demand forecast and adjust our inventory management and fulfillment processes accordingly. By addressing these key areas, we can enhance our customer experience, improve our operational efficiency, and drive long-term growth and success for our e-commerce platform.
